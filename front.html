Courses We Offer

At NIT Delhi, we provide a wide range of academic programs designed to nurture innovation and technical excellence.

B.Tech (Bachelor of Technology)

A four-year undergraduate program focusing on foundational and advanced engineering principles across various specializations:

  • Computer Science & Engineering (CSE): Emphasis on algorithms, software development, AI, and machine learning.
  • Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE): Covers embedded systems, VLSI, communication networks, and signal processing.
  • Mechanical Engineering: Focus on thermal systems, manufacturing, robotics, and design engineering.
  • Civil Engineering: Study of infrastructure, construction technology, and environmental engineering.
  • Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI&DS): Integration of AI, big data, data mining, and analytics.
  • Aerospace Engineering: Exploration of flight mechanics, aerodynamics, and space technologies.
  • VLSI Design: Specialized in semiconductor devices, chip design, and nanoelectronics.

M.Tech (Master of Technology)

A two-year postgraduate program focused on in-depth technical knowledge and research skills in specific engineering domains such as CSE, ECE, and VLSI Design.

Ph.D. Programs

Research-intensive doctoral programs offered across departments, encouraging innovation and contribution to scientific and industrial advancement.
